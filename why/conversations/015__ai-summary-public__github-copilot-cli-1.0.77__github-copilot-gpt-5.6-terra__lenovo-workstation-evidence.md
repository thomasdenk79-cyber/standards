# Technical evidence preparation

## Purpose

Prepare a reproducible technical-support evidence set for a workstation case
while keeping the current support scope separate from restricted historical
correspondence.

## Decisions

- The current support report addresses only currently observed hybrid graphics,
  memory-residency, paging, thermal and stability behaviour.
- Historical correspondence and prior-platform context are retained in a
  separate restricted local evidence collection for an authorised escalation.
- Claims about legal responsibility and court decisions are classified as
  verified law, evidence request, or unverified research lead. No unverified
  legal proposition is presented as fact.

## Result

The evidence set includes source manifests and integrity hashes, a
support-scoped technical addendum, an internal chronology, and a legal research
worklist for review by qualified procurement or legal personnel.
